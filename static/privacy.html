Privacy and deletion

Updated 5 October 2026. This hosted Media Downloader is maintained by Matěj Teplý (Majkey). Privacy and support contact: majkeylab@gmail.com.

What happens to a submitted link

The browser sends your public media URL and selected output to the backend hosted by alwaysdata. The backend contacts the source website, reads media metadata, and prepares a temporary file. It processes your IP address for abuse prevention and rate limiting. Hosting and error logs may contain IP addresses, request metadata, file identifiers, and details of failed extraction. Do not submit private links, credentials, or sensitive media.

The purposes are delivering the requested conversion and protecting this shared service from abuse. Processing needed to fulfill your request is based on providing the requested service; security and troubleshooting use the legitimate interest in operating a reliable service. No advertising, analytics, user accounts, or payment processing are implemented.

Retention and deletion

After a complete file download, the backend attempts to remove that generated file. Abandoned files below 64 MiB have a cleanup target of 10 minutes; larger files have a 30-minute target. Cleanup runs on requests and through a scheduled task. These are targets, not guaranteed secure erasure: outages, failed cleanup, or hosting backups can delay removal.

IP-based rate limits count requests in a one-hour window. IP records remain in bounded process memory until they are evicted or the process restarts. These records are separate from hosting logs. Hosting providers apply their own retention schedules. The application does not set analytics or advertising cookies, or store submitted links in local storage.

For a deletion request concerning data held by the maintainer, email the approximate request time and relevant file identifier if available. Do not email passwords or confidential media. Files already downloaded to your device must be deleted there. Support correspondence is retained only while needed to handle and document the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

Providers

GitHub Pages serves the frontend and processes technical visitor records, including IP addresses. alwaysdata hosts the conversion backend. The media source receives backend requests for the URL you supply. Their policies describe their retention and any international transfers. The maintainer does not sell submitted URLs or media.

Your rights

Contact the maintainer to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or object to processing, and portability where applicable. You may complain to the Czech data protection authority or your local authority. Hosting providers handle requests for records they control.

Use and fees

The hosted service is free, with no purchases or subscriptions and no in-app refund process. Download only material you are authorized to download. Follow the usage rules, applicable law, and source-platform terms. Nothing here removes mandatory legal rights.
